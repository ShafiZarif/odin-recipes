Lasagna

Description:

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of wide pasta sheets, rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted cheese. It is a hearty and comforting meal that’s perfect for family dinners or special occasions.

Ingredients:

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 500g ground beef (or vegetables for a vegetarian version)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 2 cups ricotta or béchamel sauce
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh basil for garnish

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions, then drain and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet, heat olive oil over medium heat. Add onion and garlic, cooking until softened.
  3. Add ground beef and cook until browned. Drain excess fat.
  4. Stir in tomato sauce and season with salt and pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  6. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  7. Layer lasagna noodles, meat sauce, ricotta or béchamel sauce, and mozzarella cheese.
  8. Repeat layers, ending with a layer of cheese on top.
  9. Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and golden.
  10. Let rest for 10 minutes before serving. Garnish with fresh basil if desired.

Enjoy your delicious homemade lasagna, perfect for a comforting meal!
